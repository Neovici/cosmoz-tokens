Re-point the brand scale from Danube blue to Slate

Adds `--cz-slate-{25-950}` primitives and maps `--cz-color-brand-{25-950}`
onto them, so every brand-derived semantic token (solid fills, brand text,
selected surfaces, focus rings) becomes a cool neutral. The `--cz-danube-*`
primitives stay available for direct use.
